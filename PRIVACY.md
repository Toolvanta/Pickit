# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

Pickit is a Chrome extension for picking colours and detecting fonts. This policy explains
what it does with your data. In short: **nothing leaves your browser.**

## What Pickit stores

When you use Pickit it saves, in your browser's extension storage:

- colours you picked, the website hostname they came from, and any CSS variable name that matched
- fonts you inspected or saved
- palettes you create
- your settings, and a pick counter used only to decide when to ask for a review

## Where it is stored

- On your device, in `chrome.storage.local`.
- If you turn on **Sync palettes and favourites** in Settings, your palettes and favourite
  colours are also stored in `chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome copies between the
  browsers where you are signed in. This goes through your Google account under Google's
  own privacy policy; Pickit's developer never sees it.

## What Pickit does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share any data.
- It has no account, analytics, tracking or advertising SDK, and it makes no network requests.
- It does not read pages you visit unless you start the colour picker, font inspector or
  page scan on that page.

## Links

Pickit's own pages contain links to its sponsor (ToolEnhancer) and to the developer's
GitHub, LinkedIn and portfolio. They open only when you click them. The sponsor link carries
`utm_source=pickit` so the sponsor can count visits from Pickit; it contains nothing about you.

## Your control

- Settings → **Clear data** removes everything Pickit stored on this device.
- Settings → **Export a backup** saves a copy of your data as a file you keep.
- Uninstalling Pickit removes all of its local data.
